# PantryPilot demo video — 2:25 target

Record in English with your own voice. Use no protected music, retailer branding or third-party footage. Keep the cursor slow and leave the Agent Trace visible during tool execution.

## Script and shot list

### 0:00–0:15 — The problem

**Picture:** PantryPilot hero and ingredient shelf.

**Voice-over:** “Dinner is not a recipe problem. It is a chain of small decisions: what do I have, what fits my time and diet, what is missing, and what should I do next? PantryPilot turns that chain into one visible, collaborative flow.”

### 0:15–0:35 — WebMCP connection

**Picture:** Open Demo Guide. Point to WebMCP readiness, Golden Prompt and the ten-tool belt.

**Voice-over:** “PantryPilot is a real WebMCP cooking app. Its ten structured tools operate the same live state as the interface. Watch the Agent Trace on the right: the agent does not guess where to click, and every action remains visible.”

### 0:35–1:25 — Golden Prompt and tool sequence

**Picture:** Choose Prepare demo, copy and submit the Golden Prompt. Keep Agent Trace in view as kitchen state, planning and substitution run.

**Voice-over:** “I reset a deterministic demo pantry and ask: plan a vegetarian dinner for two in under twenty-five minutes, use my tomatoes first, replace dairy, add anything missing, then start cooking. The agent reads my kitchen, selects Golden tomato rice, scales it for two, and swaps whole milk for oat milk. Keep your eye on the Agent Trace: it records the exact tool, compact input, result, status and duration for every action.”

### 1:25–1:50 — Human approval

**Picture:** Shopping review open; trace says `needs-user`. Pause, then approve.

**Voice-over:** “Here is the important boundary. The agent can prepare missing groceries, but it cannot approve them. The trace remains needs-user until I review the list. Only my click changes the shopping list. No tool can purchase or submit payment.”

### 1:50–2:10 — Cook and persist

**Picture:** Cooking mode, advance one step, set timer, reload the page and show restored state.

**Voice-over:** “Now the agent starts the guided cooking mode and sets a persistent timer. Steps, plan, timer and trace survive a reload, so the experience remains useful in a real kitchen.”

### 2:10–2:25 — Architecture and close

**Picture:** Brief architecture graphic or README, then return to plated recipe.

**Voice-over:** “React, Zustand and Zod provide one validated state layer for human and agent actions. PantryPilot shows how WebMCP can make everyday agents capable without making them opaque. Your kitchen, on autopilot—with you still in control.”

## Capture checklist

- Use a clean 16:9 recording at 1080p.
- Start from the public `?demo=1` URL in a WebMCP-capable browser.
- Close personal tabs and hide notifications, bookmarks and account details.
- Record one clean take of the Golden Journey before editing.
- Add simple captions for tool names and `needs-user`; avoid fast transitions.
- Confirm final runtime is below three minutes and voice is clearly audible.
- Upload publicly to YouTube and paste the URL into Devpost and `DEVPOST.md`.
